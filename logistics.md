# Logistics
1. Venue and equipment
2.Book the campus plaza or a large seminar room at least three weeks before the event.
3. Reserve a microphone, speaker and projector for the welcome and closing sessions.
4. Arrange tables and chairs for the registration area and activity stations.
5. Prepare a wet-weather backup room in case the outdoor plaza cannot be used.
6. Print name tags, attendance sheets and directional signs a few days before the event.
# Volunteers and safety
1. Recruit at least eight student volunteers for registration, guiding and cleanup.
2. Brief all volunteers one day before the event on their roles and the schedule.
3.Identify first-aid contacts and the nearest emergency exits, and share them with volunteers.
# Questions for discussion
1. How many students are expected to attend?
2. Do we need approval from the campus office to use the plaza?
# Assumption 
Refreshments will be delivered by a vendor 30 minutes before the event starts.
