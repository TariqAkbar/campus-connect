# Publicity
1. Publicity channels
2. Post an announcement on the Campus Connect social media page two weeks before the event.
3. Place printed posters at the library, canteen and main lecture block.
4. Ask student clubs to share the event link in their group chats.
5. Set up a registration table at the campus plaza during the week before the event.
6. Send a reminder message to registered students one day before the event.
# Key messages
1. Campus Connect is a free, welcoming event for students from all courses.
2. No prior experience is needed; everyone can join the activities.
3.Registration is required so that the team can plan seating and refreshments.
# Questions for discussion
1. Which social media platform do most students in our group actually use?
2. Should the poster design be done by the team or requested from a design club?

# Assumption
The event has a registration form available by the time publicity starts.
